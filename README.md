# Mayasari Bakery Analysis

Data analysis project for exploring sales, customer, product, and business performance of a bakery business.

---

## 1. Project Overview

Mayasari Bakery Analysis is a portfolio-grade data analytics project designed to transform raw bakery business data into structured information, actionable insights, and business recommendations.

The project follows a reproducible analytical workflow:

1. Data acquisition
2. Data inspection
3. Data validation
4. Data cleaning
5. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
6. Business analysis
7. Visualization
8. Insight generation
9. Business recommendations

The analysis will be refined based on the actual structure and quality of the available dataset.

---

## 2. Business Context

A bakery business generates operational data through daily transactions, products, customers, and sales activities.

This project aims to analyze that information to understand:

- Sales performance
- Revenue patterns
- Product performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Transaction trends
- Business opportunities
- Potential operational problems

The ultimate goal is to turn raw business data into evidence that can support better business decisions.

---

## 3. Objectives

### Business Objectives

- Understand overall sales performance.
- Identify the best-performing products.
- Identify products with weak performance.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify sales trends over time.
- Identify potential revenue opportunities.
- Identify potential business problems.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

### Technical Objectives

- Build a reproducible data analysis workflow.
- Apply data cleaning and validation techniques.
- Perform exploratory data analysis.
- Create meaningful visualizations.
- Document analytical decisions.
- Separate raw data from processed data.
- Produce a portfolio-ready case study.

---

## 4. Analytical Questions

The exact questions will be refined after the dataset has been inspected.

### Sales

- How much revenue is generated?
- How many transactions are recorded?
- How does revenue change over time?
- What periods generate the highest sales?
- What periods generate the lowest sales?
- What is the average transaction value?

### Products

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products are purchased most frequently?
- Which products have weak performance?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Are there products with unusually low or high sales?

### Customers

- How many customers are represented in the dataset?
- What is the purchasing frequency?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- What is the average customer spend?
- Are identifiable customer segments present?

### Business Opportunities

- Which products should receive more attention?
- Which products may require promotional strategies?
- Which periods could benefit from targeted campaigns?
- Which products or customers represent potential growth opportunities?
- What business actions can be supported by the data?
